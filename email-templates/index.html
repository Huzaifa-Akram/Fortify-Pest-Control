Sending it from Outlook

  1. 1

    Open the design in a browser

    Double-click the .html file — it opens in Chrome or Edge and shows exactly what the recipient will see.

  2. 2

    Select all, then copy

    Click on the page, press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C.

  3. 3

    Paste into a new Outlook message

    Click New Email, click into the message body, press Ctrl + V. Colours, buttons, logo and icons all come across.

  4. 4

    Personalise it

    Change “Hi there,” to their name and adjust the paragraphs. Type straight over the text — formatting holds.

  5. 5

    Add a subject line

    “Pest control for your Winnipeg property — free inspection”
    “Quick question about pest protection at [business name]”
    “Mice, wasps or bed bugs? We can help — Fortify Pest Control”

  6. 6

    Send to one person at a time

    One recipient in the To field. This is what keeps you out of spam folders.

Never put many addresses in one message

A batch of people in To or CC exposes everyone’s address to everyone else, and it’s the fastest way to get fortifypest.ca flagged as spam — which would stop normal business email reaching customers too.

Staying out of spam

  • Send one at a time and space them out. About 20–30 a day is safe from a GoDaddy mailbox; a sudden burst of 200 looks like spam.
  • Save time with a template. Paste once, then File → Save As → Outlook Template (.oft). Re-open from New Items → More Items → Choose Form.
  • Keep the unsubscribe line. Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL) requires a working opt-out on commercial email. Honour requests the same day.
  • Always send from @fortifypest.ca, never Gmail or Hotmail — the domain builds your sending reputation.
  • Ask the host to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set. One-time setup, and the single biggest factor in reaching inboxes instead of junk folders.
